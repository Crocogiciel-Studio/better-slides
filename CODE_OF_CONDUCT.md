# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Better Slide to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and welcoming, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained unwelcome contact.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they judge inappropriate.

Report a problem privately to guillaume.monrolin@crocogiciel.com. Reports are handled in confidence.
